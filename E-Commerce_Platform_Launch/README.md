## 🎯 Project Objectives

The project was designed to demonstrate how a Project Coordinator supports a cross-functional technology launch from planning through closeout.

Key objectives included:

- ✅ Coordinate project activities, owners, deadlines, and dependencies
- ✅ Maintain project schedules and milestone tracking
- ✅ Monitor risks, issues, assumptions, and dependencies

- ✅ Track project budget and variance

- ✅ Support change-control decisions

- ✅ Coordinate launch-readiness activities
- ✅ Document project outcomes and lessons learned

# ⚠️ RAID Management

The project includes structured tracking for:

- **Risks**
- **Assumptions**
- **Issues**
- **Dependencies**

Example risks and issues include:

### 💳 Payment Certification Risk

**Risk:** Payment gateway certification could delay launch.

**Mitigation:** Complete certification in staging while maintaining a backup processor option.

### 📦 Catalog Data Quality Risk

**Risk:** Incorrect catalog data could create inaccurate product listings.

**Mitigation:** Perform validation rules and sample audits before UAT.

### 🐞 UAT Checkout Defects

**Issue:** Three checkout defects were discovered during UAT.

**Response:** Daily defect triage with all critical defects resolved before launch-readiness approval.
