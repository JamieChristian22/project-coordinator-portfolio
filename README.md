### 🛒 01 — E-Commerce Platform Launch

![Domain](https://img.shields.io/badge/Domain-Digital%20Commerce-2563EB?style=flat-square)
![Role](https://img.shields.io/badge/Role-Project%20Coordinator-7C3AED?style=flat-square)
![Exercise](https://img.shields.io/badge/Exercise%20Gate-HOLD-F59E0B?style=flat-square)

**📌 Business Challenge**

Payment certification evidence arrives after the readiness cutoff. The coordinator traces payment, catalog, checkout/UAT, security, and operations dependencies, assigns follow-up, and escalates launch blockers to the appropriate approver.

**🧩 Key Responsibilities**

- 📅 Track project milestones, tasks, owners, and dependencies.
- 💳 Coordinate payment integration and certification follow-up.
- 🧪 Monitor UAT, checkout testing, and unresolved defects.
- ⚠️ Document risks, blockers, and escalation requirements.
- 📊 Prepare project dashboards and executive status updates.
- 💰 Track project costs, forecasts, and potential changes.
- 🚀 Support release-readiness review and operational handoff.

**🎯 Demonstrated Approach**

Compare parallel certification checks with a scoped fallback; track cost/schedule consequences; require checkout test evidence, no open launch-blocking defects, and support-owner acceptance before a release recommendation.

**The supplemental readiness gate remains on HOLD.**

### 🏥 02 — Hospital Intake Workflow Optimization

![Domain](https://img.shields.io/badge/Domain-Healthcare%20Operations-16A34A?style=flat-square)
![Role](https://img.shields.io/badge/Role-Project%20Coordinator-7C3AED?style=flat-square)
![Exercise](https://img.shields.io/badge/Exercise%20Gate-HOLD-F59E0B?style=flat-square)

**📌 Business Challenge**

Insurance exceptions accumulate between registration and verification. Lower average wait times alone do not mean exceptions were resolved.

**🧩 Key Responsibilities**

- 📝 Document current-state and future-state workflows.
- ⏱️ Monitor registration and patient wait-time measures.
- ⚠️ Track exceptions, blockers, and unresolved handoffs.
- 👥 Coordinate registration, verification, IT, and operations.
- 📊 Maintain project dashboards and status reporting.
- 📅 Monitor pilot milestones and action deadlines.
- 🔄 Support operational acceptance and handoff.

**🎯 Demonstrated Approach**

Coordinate pilot reviews and cross-team follow-ups, use an owner-based exception queue, track timestamps and dispositions, and compare staffing or queue-management options.

The acceptance rule requires every pilot exception to have an owner, next action, and disposition.

**The supplemental readiness gate remains on HOLD.** No real patient records or protected health information are used.

### ☁️ 03 — Salesforce CRM Implementation

![Domain](https://img.shields.io/badge/Domain-Enterprise%20SaaS-00A1E0?style=flat-square)
![Role](https://img.shields.io/badge/Role-Implementation%20Coordinator-7C3AED?style=flat-square)
![Exercise](https://img.shields.io/badge/Exercise%20Gate-HOLD-F59E0B?style=flat-square)

**📌 Business Challenge**

High training completion conceals missing role-approved user activation. Training attendance must not be treated as successful CRM access.

**🧩 Key Responsibilities**

- 📅 Coordinate implementation milestones and dependencies.
- 👥 Maintain user rosters and training coordination.
- 🔐 Track role assignments and access-readiness issues.
- 🧪 Support UAT, testing, and implementation checks.
- 📊 Monitor adoption and activation reporting.
- ⚠️ Escalate unresolved access and deployment risks.
- 🚀 Coordinate implementation handoff and support readiness.

**🎯 Demonstrated Approach**

Coordinate roster review, role mapping, training, sign-in checks, UAT follow-up, and adoption reporting.

Route access approvals to the authorized owner and assess a smaller approved cohort as a fallback.

**The supplemental readiness gate remains on HOLD** while mandatory evidence is incomplete.

**🔎 Project Evidence**

### 🔄 How Delivery Controls Connect

~~~text
📘 Business Need + Acceptance Criteria
                  ↓
📅 Workstreams + Owners + Tasks + Dependencies
                  ↓
⚠️ Issue/Risk → Action → Due Date → Escalation
                  ↓
👥 Decision Proposal → Authorized Approver
                  ↓
💰 Cost/Schedule Checks + 🧪 Acceptance Evidence
                  ↓
🚦 HOLD Until Mandatory Requirements Are Satisfied
                  ↓
🚀 Authorized Handoff → Operations Owner → Follow-Up
~~~

**Governance matters:** Project coordinators organize and escalate. A project manager, sponsor, or designated operations owner authorizes the relevant scope, risk, and release decisions.
